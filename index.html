🏫 Our School Campus

About of School

Swami Shantanand Public School is located at Nauma village, Halsi, Lakhisarai, Bihar.

Our school provides a positive, disciplined and student-friendly environment where students can learn, explore their talents and prepare for a successful future.

We believe education should develop knowledge, confidence, character, creativity and responsibility.
