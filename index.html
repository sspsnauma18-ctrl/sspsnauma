Welcome to SSPS

A modern learning environment focused on education, discipline, knowledge and overall development of students.

🏫 Our School Campus

Swami Shantanand Public School

Swami Shantanand Public School is located at Nauma village, Halsi, Lakhisarai, Bihar. Our aim is to provide students with a positive and disciplined environment where they can learn, grow and achieve their goals.

We believe that education is not only about textbooks. It is about developing knowledge, confidence, creativity, discipline and responsible citizens.
